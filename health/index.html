Emergency Warning Signs — Call Your Vet Immediately

If your dog shows any of these symptoms, do not wait. Contact your vet or an emergency animal hospital right away.

😮‍💨
Difficulty BreathingGasping, blue-tinged gums, or labored breathing at rest
🫀
Bloated or Distended BellyEspecially in large breeds — can be life-threatening bloat (GDV)
⚡
Seizures or CollapseAny seizure lasting more than 2 minutes is a medical emergency
🩸
Uncontrolled BleedingDeep cuts, internal bleeding signs, or blood in vomit / stool
🤢
Repeated Vomiting or DiarrheaMore than 2–3 times in a few hours, or with blood present
😵
Loss of ConsciousnessUnresponsive, won't wake up, or extreme sudden weakness
🧪
Known Toxin IngestionAte chocolate, grapes, xylitol, rat poison, or any medication
🌡️
Suspected HeatstrokeExcessive panting, drooling, stumbling — especially in hot weather

Quick Reference

Common Symptoms — At a Glance

A fast reference guide. Not a substitute for veterinary advice — when in doubt, always call your vet.

SymptomPossible CausesAction
🤢 Vomiting (once or twice)Ate too fast, grass, mild upsetMonitor 24 hrs
🤮 Vomiting (repeated / with blood)Obstruction, toxin, parvo, pancreatitisCall vet now
😴 Lethargy (mild)Tired, hot day, mild illnessMonitor 24–48 hrs
😴 Lethargy (sudden, severe)Infection, internal bleeding, heart issueCall vet now
🦶 Limping (sudden, mild)Paw injury, muscle strainRest, check paw, monitor
🦶 Limping (won't bear weight)Fracture, dislocation, severe injurySee vet today
🍽️ Won't eat (1–2 meals)Stress, hot weather, mild illnessMonitor, offer bland food
🍽️ Won't eat (more than 2 days)Dental pain, obstruction, serious illnessCall vet
😮‍💨 Coughing (occasional)Kennel cough, allergies, minor irritationMonitor 48 hrs
😮‍💨 Coughing + breathing difficultyHeart disease, pneumonia, tracheal collapseCall vet now

⚠️ This table is for general guidance only. Your vet is always the right call when you are unsure.
