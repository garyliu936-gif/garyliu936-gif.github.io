Emergency Warning Signs — Call Your Vet Immediately

If your dog shows any of these symptoms, do not wait — contact your vet or emergency animal hospital right away.

😮‍💨
Difficulty Breathing Gasping, blue-tinged gums, or labored breathing at rest
🫀
Bloated or Distended Belly Especially in large breeds — can be life-threatening bloat (GDV)
⚡
Seizures or Collapse Any seizure lasting more than 2 minutes is a medical emergency
🩸
Uncontrolled Bleeding Deep cuts, internal bleeding signs, or blood in vomit / stool
🤢
Repeated Vomiting or Diarrhea More than 2–3 times in a few hours, or with blood present
😵
Loss of Consciousness Unresponsive, won't wake up, or extreme sudden weakness
🧪
Known Toxin Ingestion Ate chocolate, grapes, xylitol, rat poison, or any medication
🌡️
Suspected Heatstroke Excessive panting, drooling, stumbling — especially in hot weather

Quick Reference

Common Symptoms — At a Glance

A fast reference guide. Not a substitute for veterinary advice — when in doubt, call your vet.

Symptom Possible Causes Action
🤢 Vomiting (once or twice) Ate too fast, grass, mild upset Monitor 24 hrs
🤮 Vomiting (repeated / with blood) Obstruction, toxin, parvo, pancreatitis Call vet now
😴 Lethargy (mild) Tired after exercise, hot day, mild illness Monitor 24–48 hrs
😴 Lethargy (sudden, severe) Infection, internal bleeding, heart issue Call vet now
🦶 Limping (sudden, mild) Paw injury, muscle strain Rest, check paw, monitor
🦶 Limping (won't bear weight) Fracture, dislocation, severe injury See vet today
🍽️ Won't eat (1–2 meals) Stress, hot weather, mild illness Monitor, offer bland food
🍽️ Won't eat (more than 2 days) Dental pain, obstruction, serious illness Call vet
😮‍💨 Coughing (occasional) Kennel cough, allergies, minor irritation Monitor 48 hrs
😮‍💨 Coughing + breathing difficulty Heart disease, pneumonia, tracheal collapse Call vet now

⚠️ This table is for general guidance only. Your vet is always the right call when you are unsure.
