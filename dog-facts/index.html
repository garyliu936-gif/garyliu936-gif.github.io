Quick Trivia

Did You Know?

A handful of facts to get you started — the full stories are just a click away.

15,000

Years ago, dogs became the first animal ever domesticated by humans — long before cats, cattle, or horses.

300M

Scent receptors in a dog's nose, compared to about 6 million in a human — dogs can literally smell time passing.

29

Years, 5 months — the officially verified lifespan of Bobi, the oldest dog ever recorded.

#1

Space traveler: Laika, a Moscow stray, became the first animal to orbit the Earth in 1957.
