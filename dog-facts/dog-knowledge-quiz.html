How Much Do You Really Know About Dogs?

10 questions on breeds, history, biology, and behavior. No signup, no time limit — just see how you score.

🧠 10 questions · 3 min
Question 1 of 10

A dog's nose has roughly how many more scent receptors than a human's?

A dog's nose has around 300 million scent receptors versus a human's roughly 6 million — about 50 times more.
Question 2 of 10

On average, how many hours a day does an adult dog sleep?

Dogs sleep an average of 12–14 hours a day — puppies and senior dogs often sleep even more.
Question 3 of 10

Which breed is nicknamed the "barkless dog" for its yodel-like howl?

The Basenji can't bark in the traditional sense — instead it makes a unique yodel-like sound nicknamed a "baroo."
Question 4 of 10

How many adult teeth does a dog have?

Dogs grow 42 adult teeth by around 6–7 months old — 10 more than an adult human's 32.
Question 5 of 10

Which breed holds the record as the fastest dog over short distances?

Greyhounds can reach speeds up to 45 mph, making them the fastest dog breed over short distances.
Question 6 of 10

What's actually true about tail wagging?

Tail wagging is a whole language — direction, speed, and height all change the meaning, and a wag can signal anything from joy to anxiety to a warning.
Question 7 of 10

Which breed has six toes on each foot, an adaptation for climbing cliffs?

The Norwegian Lundehund has six toes per foot — an adaptation for climbing steep, narrow cliffs to hunt puffins.
Question 8 of 10

Roughly how long ago were dogs first domesticated from wolves?

Dogs were domesticated from wolves roughly 15,000 years ago, making them the first domesticated animal — well before cats, cattle, or horses.
Question 9 of 10

Which dog was the first living creature to orbit the Earth?

Laika, a Moscow stray, became the first living creature to orbit the Earth aboard Sputnik 2 in 1957.
Question 10 of 10

What's the oldest verified age a dog has ever lived to?

Bobi, a Portuguese dog, holds the officially verified record at 29 years and 5 months old.
