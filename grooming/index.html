Grooming by Coat

Know Your Dog's Coat Type

Different coats need different care. Find your dog's coat type to understand their grooming needs.

🐕

Short / Smooth Coat

Easy to maintain — minimal brushing, occasional baths. Moderate shedding despite short hair.

Labrador, Beagle, Dalmatian, Boxer Brush weekly
🐺

Double Coat

Heavy seasonal shedding twice a year. Never shave — it damages the coat and disrupts temperature regulation.

Husky, Corgi, GSD, Malamute Brush 3× / week
✨

Long Silky Coat

Tangles and mats easily. Needs daily brushing and regular trims to stay clean and comfortable.

Shih Tzu, Maltese, Yorkie, Afghan Brush daily
🐩

Curly / Wavy Coat

Low-shedding but high-maintenance. Mats form quickly without regular brushing. Professional cuts every 6–8 weeks.

Poodle, Doodles, Bichon, Cockapoo Brush 4× / week
🦔

Wire / Rough Coat

Needs hand-stripping or clipping every few months. Dead coat must be removed to keep texture and shape.

Wire Fox Terrier, Dachshund (wire), Griffon Strip every 3 months
🐑

Corded Coat

Forms natural dreadlock-style cords. Requires careful separation when young and months of maintenance to develop properly.

Puli, Komondor, Bergamasco Separate cords weekly
Quick Reference

Grooming Frequency by Coat Type

A fast reference guide for bath, brush, nail trim, and ear-cleaning schedules.

Coat Type Bath Frequency Brushing Nail / Ear Check
🐕 Short / Smooth Monthly Weekly Nails monthly · Ears monthly
🐺 Double Coat Every 6–8 weeks 2–3× per week Nails monthly · Ears monthly
✨ Long Silky Every 3–4 weeks Daily Nails every 3 weeks · Ears bi-weekly
🐩 Curly / Wavy Every 3–4 weeks 4× per week Nails every 3 weeks · Ears bi-weekly
🦔 Wire / Rough Every 6–8 weeks Weekly Nails monthly · Ears monthly
🐑 Corded Every 4–6 weeks Cord separation weekly Nails monthly · Ears bi-weekly

⚠️ Schedules vary by individual dog, activity level, and climate. Adjust as needed for your dog.
