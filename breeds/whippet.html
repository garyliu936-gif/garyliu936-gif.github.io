Feeding Your Whippet

Whippets typically eat 1.5–2.5 cups of high-quality dry kibble per day, split into two meals. Their lean build means it can be hard to tell if they're at an ideal weight — a slight waist and visible last two ribs are normal. Avoid overfeeding as excess weight stresses their frame.

Nutrition Tips

  • High-quality protein supports their lean, athletic muscle mass
  • Avoid overfeeding — a slightly lean-looking Whippet is healthier than an overweight one
  • Omega-3 fatty acids support skin and coat health
  • Sensitive stomachs are common — gradual food transitions if changing brands
  • Active Whippets competing in lure coursing or racing may need higher caloric intake

Foods to Avoid

  • Grapes and raisins (toxic)
  • Onions and garlic (toxic)
  • Chocolate (toxic)
  • High-fat or high-sugar treats (unnecessary calories)

Popular Whippet Mixes

Whipador (Whippet + Labrador Retriever)

The Whipador combines the Whippet's elegant speed with the Lab's friendly, outgoing personality. A medium-to-large dog, the Whipador tends to be athletic, social, and highly trainable — a wonderful active family dog.

Whippet-Border Collie Mix

An athletic, brainy combination — this mix brings the Whippet's speed together with the Border Collie's legendary intelligence and herding instinct. High-energy and highly capable, this mix excels in dog sports like agility.

Pitwhip (Whippet + Pit Bull Terrier)

The Pitwhip is an athletic, muscular mix combining the Whippet's lean grace with the Pit Bull's powerful build. Often a loving, energetic companion with a streamlined build and strong prey drive — needs proper socialization.

Whippet-Greyhound Mix

Two sighthound breeds combined into one supremely fast, elegant dog. This mix shares all the best sighthound traits — quiet indoors, explosive outdoors, gentle with family, and deeply affectionate.

Fun Facts About Whippets

  • 🏃 World's Fastest Medium Dog: Whippets can reach speeds of 35 mph (56 km/h) — making them the fastest dog in their weight class. They regularly beat much larger breeds in short-distance racing.
  • 🏭 Working Class Origins: Whippets were originally bred by English coal miners in the 1800s who couldn't afford to keep Greyhounds. They were raced in makeshift tracks on weekends — becoming known as "the poor man's racehorse."
  • 😴 Marathon Sleepers: Don't mistake the Whippet's legendary laziness indoors for a lack of ability — they can sleep 16+ hours a day and then immediately reach full sprint speed in less than a second.
  • 🤫 Nearly Silent: Whippets are one of the least vocal dog breeds. They rarely bark without reason, making them surprisingly good apartment dogs despite their athletic physique.
  • 🥶 Cold Haters: With virtually zero body fat and a paper-thin coat, Whippets genuinely suffer in cold weather. Many dedicated Whippet owners have extensive dog coat wardrobes for their pets.
  • 🐱 Cat Personality: Whippets are frequently described as having cat-like tendencies — they groom themselves, seek warmth obsessively, and will curl into impossibly small spaces to stay comfortable.
  • 🎨 Most Colorful Breed: Whippets come in nearly every color and pattern recognized in dogs — solids, brindles, parti-colors, piebald, and more. No two Whippets look exactly alike.
