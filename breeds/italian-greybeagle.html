Italian Greybeagle

Designer Mixed Breed - Italian Greyhound x Beagle - Speedy Scent Hound

Overview

The Italian Greybeagle combines the Italian Greyhound speed and elegance with the Beagle sociability and nose. The result is a sleek, friendly scent hound that is loving at home but stubbornly distracted outdoors. Always leash required.

Temperament

Friendly, curious, and energetic. The Beagle brings warmth and nose; the Italian Greyhound brings speed and sensitivity. Social and loving at home.

  • Friendly with everyone
  • Curious and scent-driven
  • Fast and agile
  • Can be stubborn

Exercise

Moderate to high - 45-60 minutes daily. Needs enclosed space for running. Scent following means leash is mandatory outside fenced areas.

Grooming

Very easy - short smooth coat needs minimal brushing. Wipe down weekly is usually sufficient.

Training

Challenging - two scent hound types with independent natures. Food motivation helps but patience and consistency are essential.

Health

Leg fractures from the Italian Greyhound side are a concern. Ear infections from Beagle side. Generally healthy overall.

Speed Meets Nose

Italian Greyhounds are one of the fastest small breeds; Beagles have one of the most powerful noses in the dog world. Italian Greybeagles combine speed and scent in a unique package.

Musical Dogs

Beagles are famous for their melodious howls. Italian Greybeagles often inherit this vocalization and will sing enthusiastically when excited or tracking an interesting scent.
