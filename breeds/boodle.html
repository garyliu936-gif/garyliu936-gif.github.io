Overview

The Boodle pairs the lovable, gentle Bulldog with the brilliant Poodle. The result is a compact, friendly companion that combines the Bulldog's calm affection with the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat. Boodles are adaptable, great with families, and significantly easier to train than purebred Bulldogs.

Temperament

Calm, affectionate, and friendly. More energetic than a purebred Bulldog but calmer than a Poodle. Loves people of all ages.

  • Gentle and friendly with everyone
  • Excellent with children
  • Adaptable to apartment or house
  • Sociable with other pets

Exercise

Moderate needs - 30-45 minutes daily. Be careful in heat due to Bulldog's brachycephalic traits. Short, cool walks are best in summer.

Grooming

Wavy coat needs brushing 2-3 times weekly. Clean facial folds from Bulldog side daily to prevent skin infections.

Training

Much more trainable than a purebred Bulldog thanks to Poodle intelligence. Responds well to positive reinforcement and food rewards.

Health

Brachycephalic issues from Bulldog side are the primary concern. Hip dysplasia and skin fold infections also common.

Fun Facts

😴

Expert Loungers

Boodles combine the Bulldog legendary love of napping with occasional Poodle bursts of play energy. They do both enthusiastically.

🌡️

Heat Sensitive

The Bulldog facial structure means Boodles should never be overexerted in warm weather. Early morning or evening walks in summer.

🧠

Smarter than They Look

Despite the stocky Bulldog appearance, Poodle intelligence shines through. Boodles can learn impressive trick repertoires.
