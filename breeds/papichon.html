Papichon

Designer Mixed Breed - Papillon x Bichon Frise - Elegant White Companion

8-14 lbsWeight
9-13 inHeight

Overview

The Papichon combines the Papillon intelligence and elegance with the Bichon Frise warmth and low-shedding coat. A charming, friendly, and highly trainable companion that is excellent for allergy sufferers and apartment dwellers. One of the most well-rounded small hybrids available.

Temperament

Friendly, intelligent, and social. Papillon elegance and intelligence combined with Bichon warmth creates a perfect small companion for virtually any lifestyle.

  • Excellent with everyone
  • Highly intelligent
  • Playful and gentle
  • Low shedding

Exercise

Moderate - 30-40 minutes daily. Indoor play and walks perfectly satisfy this adaptable small companion.

Grooming

Soft coat needs brushing 3-4 times weekly and professional grooming every 6-8 weeks.

Training

Very trainable - Papillon intelligence with Bichon eagerness creates a highly responsive small dog. Excellent for tricks and agility.

Health

Generally healthy small breeds. Dental disease, luxating patella, and eye conditions are primary watch points.

Fun Facts About Papichon

🎏

French Elegance Doubled

Both Papillons and Bichon Frises are French-associated breeds with aristocratic histories as companion dogs of European nobility. Papichons carry this elegant heritage.

💨

Allergy Friendly

Bichon Frises are one of the most recommended breeds for allergy sufferers. Combined with the Papillon low-shedding coat, Papichons produce minimal pet dander.
