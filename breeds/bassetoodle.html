Bassetoodle

Overview

The Bassetoodle blends the Basset Hound's friendly, laid-back personality with the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat. This charming hybrid is great with families and adapts well to various living situations, combining the Basset's scent drive with Poodle smarts.

Origin
United States

Not AKC registered (mixed breed)
Coat Type
Wavy to curly; low to moderate shedding

Temperament

Sweet, friendly, and moderately energetic. The Poodle's intelligence makes Bassetoodles more trainable than purebred Bassets. Social and affectionate.

  • Friendly with everyone
  • Good with children
  • More trainable than purebred Basset
  • Scent drive remains strong

Exercise

Moderate exercise - 30-45 minutes daily. Enjoys sniff walks and playtime. Watch weight - both parent breeds can be prone to obesity.

Grooming

Wavy coat needs brushing 2-3 times weekly. Professional grooming every 8-10 weeks. Clean long ears weekly.

Training

More trainable than a Basset thanks to Poodle intelligence. Use food rewards and keep sessions short. Early socialization important.

Health

IVDD (back problems) from the Basset and ear infections are primary concerns. Regular weight monitoring recommended.

Fun Facts About Bassetoodle

🧠

Smart Hound

Bassetoodles are significantly more trainable than purebred Bassets thanks to Poodle intelligence. They can learn impressive trick repertoires.

🦮

Low-Shedding Scent Dog

If you love scent hounds but hate dog hair, the Bassetoodle may be your answer - the Poodle coat drastically reduces shedding.

👂

Those Ears

Bassetoodles nearly always inherit the Basset long, floppy ears. They require weekly cleaning to prevent infections.
