Breed Overview

The Chinese Shar-Pei is one of the world's most distinctive-looking dogs, instantly recognizable by its deep wrinkles and hippo-like face. The name "Shar-Pei" means "sand-paper skin" in Cantonese, referring to their rough, bristly coat. An ancient Chinese breed used as guard dogs, hunters, and herders, they were once listed in the Guinness World Records as one of the rarest dog breeds on earth.

Shar-Peis are devoted and loyal to their families but naturally aloof with strangers. They are intelligent and independent — sometimes described as cat-like in their self-sufficiency. While calm indoors, they are surprisingly agile and watchful. Early socialization is essential to develop a well-adjusted, confident temperament.

Quick Facts

  • AKC Rank: #66 most popular
  • Group: Non-Sporting
  • Origin: China
  • Size: Medium
  • Coat: Short, harsh ("horse" or "brush" coat)
  • Tongue color: Blue-black (unique to Shar-Pei and Chow)
  • Shedding: Low–Moderate
  • Good for Apartments: Yes (with daily walks)

Health Notes

  • Shar-Pei Fever (FSF) — autoinflammatory condition unique to the breed
  • Skin fold dermatitis — wrinkles need regular cleaning
  • Entropion — eyelids rolling inward (common)
  • Amyloidosis — kidney disease related to FSF

Feeding Your Chinese Shar-Pei

Shar-Peis can have sensitive skin and digestive systems. A limited-ingredient or novel protein diet often helps manage allergies common in the breed.

Life StageDaily AmountCalories/Day
Puppy (under 12 mo)1.5–2.5 cups700–1,100 kcal
Adult (1–7 yrs)2–2.5 cups900–1,200 kcal
Senior (7+ yrs)1.5–2 cups700–950 kcal

Nutrition Tips

  • Novel proteins (duck, venison, rabbit) reduce allergic reactions
  • Omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids support skin health within the wrinkles
  • Avoid corn, wheat, and soy if skin issues are present
  • Keep fresh water always available — Shar-Peis can overheat quickly

Fun Facts About Chinese Shar-Peis

  • 📚 In 1978, a Hong Kong businessman published an appeal in LIFE magazine to save the nearly-extinct Shar-Pei. American breeders responded and saved the breed.
  • 👅 Shar-Peis and Chow Chows are the only two dog breeds with a naturally blue-black tongue — a trait considered a sign of purebred lineage.
  • 🌊 Shar-Pei puppies are so covered in loose skin and wrinkles they look like they're wearing a suit several sizes too large — the wrinkles tighten somewhat as they grow.
  • 🏛️ Chinese pottery and statues dating back to the Han Dynasty (206 BC–220 AD) depict dogs remarkably similar to the Shar-Pei.
  • 🦠 Shar-Pei Fever (FSF) is a unique autoimmune condition found almost exclusively in this breed — research on it has contributed to understanding human autoinflammatory diseases.
