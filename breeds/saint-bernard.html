Feeding Your Saint Bernard

Adult Saint Bernards typically eat 5–8 cups of high-quality giant breed dry kibble per day, split into at least two or three meals. Giant breed puppy formula is critical during growth — it controls the calcium/phosphorus ratio that affects bone development.

Nutrition Tips

  • Always use giant/large breed puppy formula — regular puppy food can cause dangerous overgrowth
  • Never feed one large meal per day — split into 2–3 meals to reduce bloat risk
  • Glucosamine and chondroitin supplements support joint health under the heavy frame
  • Omega-3 fatty acids support heart, joint, and coat health
  • Watch weight carefully — even a few extra pounds is significant stress on giant breed joints

Foods to Avoid

  • Grapes and raisins (toxic)
  • Onions and garlic (toxic)
  • Chocolate (toxic)
  • Gas-producing foods (reduces bloat risk)
  • High-fat table scraps (pancreatitis risk)

Giant breed expenses are substantial. All veterinary medications, anesthesia, and surgical procedures are dosed by weight — a Saint Bernard's costs can be 3–5x those of a medium dog for the same procedure. Pet insurance is strongly recommended.

Lifetime Cost Estimate

Over a 9-year lifespan, expect to spend $18,000–$45,000+ total, especially with potential orthopedic or cardiac care.

Popular Saint Bernard Mixes

Saint Berdoodle (Saint Bernard + Poodle)

The Saint Berdoodle combines the Saint's gentle giant personality with the Poodle's intelligence and potentially reduced shedding. One of the most popular giant doodle mixes — affectionate, friendly, and often more manageable in terms of drool than a purebred Saint.

Saint Bernese (Saint Bernard + Bernese Mountain Dog)

Two Swiss mountain breeds combine in the Saint Bernese — a massive, tri-colored gentle giant with the Saint's patience and the Bernese's striking coat pattern. Typically calmer than both parents, with a deeply loyal family temperament.

Saint Lab (Saint Bernard + Labrador)

The Saint Lab blends the Lab's friendly, trainable personality with the Saint's gentle giant nature. Typically large, affectionate, and excellent with children — often more energetic than a purebred Saint and somewhat easier to train.

Saintweiler (Saint Bernard + Rottweiler)

A powerful, loyal mix of two historically working breeds. The Saintweiler tends to be a calm, protective family dog — typically inheriting the Saint's patience with the Rottweiler's confident, watchful nature. Requires experienced handling.

Fun Facts About Saint Bernards

  • 🏔️ Real Rescuers: The dogs at the Great St. Bernard Hospice in Switzerland are documented to have rescued more than 2,000 travelers from the Alpine passes between the early 1700s and mid-1800s. Their ability to locate buried avalanche victims was extraordinary.
  • 🪣 The Barrel Myth: The iconic image of a Saint Bernard carrying a small barrel of brandy around its neck is fictional — popularized by an 1820 painting by Edwin Landseer. The real rescue dogs carried no such barrels. The tradition was never part of their actual work.
  • 🐕 Barry the Legend: The most famous Saint Bernard, Barry (1800–1814), is credited with saving between 40–100 lives during his work at the hospice. His preserved body is displayed in Berne's Natural History Museum. A statue at the Cimetière des Chiens near Paris honors his memory.
  • 🎬 Beethoven: The 1992 film "Beethoven" and its sequels starring a lovable Saint Bernard introduced a new generation to the breed — and reportedly caused a surge in Saint Bernard puppy sales followed by a wave of shelter surrenders when owners underestimated the reality of owning a 150-lb drooling giant.
  • 📏 Record-Setters: The heaviest Saint Bernard on record weighed 357 lbs. While exceptional, Saints regularly reach 200 lbs in some breeding lines — among the heaviest dog breeds in the world.
  • ❄️ Snow Search Dogs: Saints were said to be able to detect travelers buried under feet of snow using their extraordinary sense of smell — and would then lie on top of them to share body heat while a companion dog returned to the hospice for help.
  • 🍺 Swiss Icon: The Saint Bernard is considered a cultural symbol of Switzerland, alongside the Swiss Army Knife, watches, and chocolate. Several hospices in the Swiss Alps still keep Saint Bernards as living heritage animals today.
