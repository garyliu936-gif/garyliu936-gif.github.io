Miniature Puggle

Designer Mixed Breed - Pug x Beagle (Mini) - Tiny and Charming

8-13 inHeight

Overview

The Miniature Puggle is a smaller version of the popular Puggle (Pug x Beagle) cross. Charming, social, and compact, the Mini Puggle is an ideal apartment companion with a big personality. The Pug side adds warmth and brachycephalic concerns while the Beagle side adds curiosity and nose.

Temperament

Friendly, curious, and lovable. A smaller version of the Puggle personality - social, food-motivated, and great with families of all sizes.

  • Very social and friendly
  • Excellent apartment dog
  • Food motivated
  • Curious nose

Exercise

Low to moderate - 20-30 minutes daily. Avoid heat and overexertion from Pug brachycephalic traits. Great for less active owners.

Grooming

Easy - short coat needs weekly brushing. Clean facial folds from Pug side daily to prevent skin infections.

Training

Moderate trainability. Food motivation helps significantly. Short engaging sessions work best as both parent breeds have short attention spans.

Health

Brachycephalic concerns from Pug side are manageable in mini puggles but still require attention. Ear infections from Beagle side are common.

Fun Facts About Miniature Puggle

📦

Pocket Sized

Miniature Puggles pack the full Puggle personality into a 10-20 lb package - perfect for people who love the Puggle temperament but want an even more compact companion.

👃

Sniff Everything

Even in a mini package, the Beagle nose is powerful. Miniature Puggles will investigate every smell on every walk with remarkable thoroughness.
