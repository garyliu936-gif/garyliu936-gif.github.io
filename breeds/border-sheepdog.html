Overview

The Border Sheepdog crosses two of the most intelligent herding breeds: the Border Collie and Shetland Sheepdog. This hybrid is exceptionally smart, athletic, and trainable - but demands an active, mentally stimulating lifestyle. Not for casual owners.

Temperament

Brilliant, energetic, and loyal. One of the most trainable hybrid dogs but also one of the most demanding mentally.

  • Exceptionally intelligent
  • Loyal and devoted to family
  • May try to herd children and small animals
  • Sensitive and attuned to owners' emotions
  • Can develop anxiety without mental stimulation

Exercise

Very high needs - 90+ minutes daily of vigorous exercise plus mental stimulation. Agility, herding, and dog sports are ideal.

Grooming

Brush 3-4 times weekly. Professional grooming every 8-10 weeks. Moderate seasonal shedding.

Training

Extremely trainable - one of the top hybrids for obedience, agility, and trick training. Use positive reinforcement. Keep challenges coming.

Health

Generally very healthy. Eye conditions from both Collie and Sheltie parents are the primary concerns.

Fun Facts

🧠

Double Intelligence

Border Collies are often ranked as the smartest dog breed, and Shelties are in the top five. Border Sheepdogs may be the most intelligent hybrid dogs possible.

🐑

Natural Herders

Both parents are dedicated herding dogs. Border Sheepdogs will try to herd anything that moves - children, cats, and even their owners.

🏆

Agility Champions

This cross dominates agility competitions. Their combination of intelligence, athleticism, and biddability is hard to match.
