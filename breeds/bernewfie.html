Overview

The Bernewfie crosses two of the gentlest giants in the dog world: the Bernese Mountain Dog and the Newfoundland. The result is an enormous, sweet-natured dog that is extraordinarily patient with children, calm with other animals, and devoted to its family. Size management is the primary challenge.

Temperament

Extraordinarily gentle and patient. One of the best family dogs in existence if you have the space. Calm, loving, and patient with children of all ages.

  • Exceptionally gentle with children
  • Calm and patient temperament
  • Loves everyone - people and animals
  • Devoted and family-focused

Exercise

Moderate exercise needs despite huge size. 45-60 minutes daily. Loves swimming - both parents are water dogs. Avoid overexertion in heat.

Grooming

Heavy grooming commitment. Brush 3-4 times weekly, daily during heavy shed periods. Professional grooming every 8-10 weeks.

Training

Intelligent and willing to please from both sides. Gentle, positive approach works best. Early training essential given their eventual size.

Health

Giant breeds carry significant joint, bloat, and heart risks. Shorter lifespan is a reality of giant breed crosses.

Fun Facts About Bernewfie

💛

Nanny Dogs

Bernewfies are among the most trusted dogs with children of all sizes. Their patience and gentleness is extraordinary even by dog standards.

🌊

Born to Swim

Both Bernese Mountain Dogs and Newfoundlands are natural swimmers with water-resistant coats. Bernewfies typically love water.

🏔️

Mountain Heritage

Both parent breeds have Swiss and Canadian mountain working heritage. Bernewfies carry this strength and endurance beautifully.
