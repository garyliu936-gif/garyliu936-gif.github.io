Breed Overview

The Belgian Tervuren is one of four varieties of Belgian Shepherd (along with the Malinois, Groenendael, and Laekenois). Distinguished by their rich fawn-to-mahogany coat with black overlay and elegant, flowing mane, Tervurens combine striking beauty with exceptional working ability. They are named after the Belgian village of Tervuren.

Highly intelligent and responsive, Tervurens excel in obedience, herding, agility, schutzhund, and search-and-rescue. They form intense bonds with their handlers and are not dogs for passive owners — they thrive on having a job to do and a person who engages them mentally and physically every day. They can be aloof with strangers and sensitive to harsh treatment.

Feeding Your Belgian Tervuren

Active and athletic, Tervurens need a high-quality diet that supports their energy levels and muscular build. Avoid overfeeding — they should maintain a lean, athletic physique.

Life StageDaily AmountCalories/Day
Puppy (under 12 mo)2–3 cups900–1,200 kcal
Adult (1–8 yrs)2.5–3.5 cups1,100–1,500 kcal
Senior (8+ yrs)2–2.5 cups850–1,100 kcal

Nutrition Tips

  • High-protein diet (28%+) supports working muscle mass
  • Joint supplements (glucosamine, fish oil) from middle age onward
  • Feed twice daily to avoid bloat risk
  • Adjust portions based on activity level — working dogs need more

Fun Facts About Belgian Tervurens

  • 🏅 Belgian Tervurens served in both World Wars as messengers, ambulance dogs, and light machine-gun cart pullers — their intelligence and courage were invaluable.
  • 🎬 The Belgian Shepherd varieties (including Tervuren) have appeared in numerous films and TV shows, often cast as German Shepherds due to their similar appearance.
  • 🌍 In most of the world, all four Belgian Shepherd varieties are considered one breed with different coat types. Only the US AKC treats them as separate breeds.
  • 🥇 Tervurens consistently place at the top of national obedience and agility competitions, despite being far less common than Border Collies and Golden Retrievers.
  • 🦊 Their rich mahogany coat with black overlay gives them a striking wolf-like appearance — the black mask and ears darken with age, adding to their dramatic look.
