🐾 Overview

The Afghan Dane combines the Afghan Hound's elegance with the Great Dane's gentle giant personality. Enormous yet graceful, this cross is calm indoors but needs experienced handling due to its size.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Calm and dignified indoors, loyal and affectionate with family, reserved with strangers. Strong prey drive.

  • Gentle giant temperament
  • Loyal to family
  • Reserved with strangers
  • Not for first-time owners

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

Moderate exercise - 45-60 minutes daily. Avoid overexertion in puppies to protect joints.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Brush 2-3 times weekly. Wipe facial wrinkles and eyes regularly.

🎓 Training

Start early - a 130 lb untrained dog is unmanageable. The Dane side helps trainability but Afghan independence requires consistency.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Giant breeds carry elevated bloat risk. Feed from raised bowl and avoid exercise right after meals.

Diet & Feeding Guide

Feed the Afghan Dane a high-quality, age-appropriate food suited to their size and activity.

Life StageDaily AmountNotes
Puppy (2-12 mo)1-2.5 cups/dayPuppy formula; 3 meals/day
Adult (1-7 yr)1.5-3 cups/day2 meals/day; adjust for activity
Senior (8+ yr)Reduce 10-20%Joint-support formula

Hydration & Treats

Always provide fresh water. Treats should be under 10% of daily calories. Avoid chocolate, grapes, onions, and xylitol.

Incredible Height

Speed Plus Size

Despite their bulk, Afghan Danes can reach 35+ mph from the Afghan parent.
