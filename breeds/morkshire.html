Morkshire

Designer Mixed Breed - Maltese x Yorkshire Terrier - Feisty Little Companion

4-8 lbsWeight
6-9 inHeight
13-16 yrsLifespan
White, Black and Tan, PartiColors

Overview

The Morkshire (commonly called Morkie) combines the Maltese gentle sweetness with the Yorkshire Terrier spirited personality. This popular tiny companion is affectionate, playful, and low-shedding - great for adults and families with older children who can handle a fragile dog gently.

Origin
United States

Coat

Temperament

Affectionate, spirited, and devoted. The Maltese sweetness with Yorkie confidence creates a lovable companion with a surprisingly big attitude.

  • Very affectionate
  • Feisty and confident
  • Loyal to family
  • Low shedding

Exercise

Low needs - 15-25 minutes daily. Indoor play and short walks are perfect for this tiny companion.

Grooming

Silky coat needs brushing 4-5 times weekly to prevent tangles. Professional grooming every 6-8 weeks. Many owners keep a shorter puppy cut for easier maintenance.

Training

Responsive to gentle positive training. The Yorkie stubborn streak can emerge - patience and consistency required. Never use harsh corrections.

Health

Dental disease is serious for both parent breeds. Luxating patella, tracheal collapse, and hypoglycemia in tiny individuals are primary health concerns.

Fun Facts About Morkshire

🐀

Rat Hunter Heritage

Yorkshire Terriers were bred to hunt rats in Yorkshire mines. Morkshires often carry this prey drive and hunting instinct in their tiny feisty package.

🏆

Toy Dog Royalty

Both Maltese and Yorkshire Terriers have histories as prized companion dogs of nobility and high society. Morkshires are genuinely aristocratic little dogs.
