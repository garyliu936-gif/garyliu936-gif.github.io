🐾 Overview

The Romanian Bucovina Shepherd (Ciobănesc Românesc de Bucovina) is a large, powerful livestock guardian originating from the Bucovina region of Romania. Bred for centuries to protect flocks from wolves and bears in the Carpathian Mountains, this fearless breed combines strength, intelligence, and a calm, devoted temperament.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Calm, courageous, and deeply loyal. The Bucovina Shepherd is gentle with family and livestock but will fiercely defend against threats. Independent by nature, they bond strongly with their handler and territory.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

Moderate energy: 45–60 minutes daily. Prefers large open spaces. Suited for rural or suburban environments with a yard.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Thick double coat. Brush 2–3 times per week; more frequently during seasonal shedding. Bathe as needed.

🎓 Training

Intelligent but independent. Early socialization and consistent, firm training are essential. Best with experienced dog owners.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally robust. Hip and elbow dysplasia screening recommended for large breeds. Bloat awareness advised.

🎉 Fun Facts About Romanian Bucovina Shepherd

  • Named after the Bucovina region of northeastern Romania.
  • Used for centuries to guard flocks against wolves and bears.
  • The FCI officially recognized the breed in 2005.
  • One of three FCI-recognized Romanian shepherd breeds.
  • Relatively rare outside Romania and Eastern Europe.
