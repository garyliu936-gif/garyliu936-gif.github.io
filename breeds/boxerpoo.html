Boxerpoo

Designer Mixed Breed - Boxer x Poodle - Playful and Smart

30-70 lbsWeight
16-24 inHeight
10-14 yrsLifespan
Fawn, Brindle, Apricot, Black, WhiteColors

Overview

The Boxerpoo combines the Boxer's clown personality and loyalty with the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat. Playful, smart, and affectionate, Boxerpoos excel as family dogs and are significantly easier to groom than many other breeds.

Photo Gallery

Temperament

Playful, affectionate, and intelligent. The Boxer fun personality dominates this cross. Excellent with children of all ages.

  • Extremely playful and entertaining
  • Excellent with children
  • Social and friendly
  • Intelligent and trainable

Exercise

High energy - 60-75 minutes daily. Loves fetch, agility, and interactive games. Mental stimulation equally important.

Grooming

Wavy coat needs brushing 2-3 times weekly. Professional grooming every 8-10 weeks.

Training

Very trainable - Poodle intelligence with Boxer enthusiasm. Positive reinforcement and food rewards work well.

Health

Hybrid vigor helps but both parents have known health issues. Brachycephalic traits from Boxer are typically mild in this cross.

Hip DysplasiaBrachycephalic Issues (mild)Heart ConditionsAddisons Disease
