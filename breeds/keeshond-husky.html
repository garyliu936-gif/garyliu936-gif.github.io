Overview

The Keeshond Husky combines two striking Arctic Spitz breeds with similar appearances but different personalities. The Keeshond brings warmth and companionship while the Husky brings endurance and independence. Beautiful, energetic, and social - a Spitz lover dream.

Temperament

Sociable, energetic, and beautiful. The Keeshond brings warm companionship; the Husky brings independence and drive. Both are vocal and expressive Arctic breeds.

  • Highly sociable
  • Energetic and active
  • Very vocal
  • Striking appearance

Exercise

High energy - 60-75 minutes of vigorous exercise daily. Both breeds are working Spitz types that need substantial daily activity.

Grooming

Very heavy grooming - plush double coats require brushing 4-5 times weekly and daily during seasonal blow. Professional grooming recommended twice yearly.

Training

Both breeds are intelligent but independent. Positive consistency from puppyhood is essential. Recall training in open areas requires significant work.

Health

Hip dysplasia, eye conditions, and Addison disease from Keeshond side are primary concerns. Generally healthy and long-lived.

Blow Season Reality

Both parent breeds have thick double coats that blow twice yearly. Keeshond Huskies produce extraordinary amounts of fluff during shedding season - enough to fill pillows.

Dutch Heritage

The Keeshond is the national dog of the Netherlands, historically kept on barges as companions. Combined with Husky Arctic heritage, the Keeshond Husky is a remarkable cross of working traditions.
