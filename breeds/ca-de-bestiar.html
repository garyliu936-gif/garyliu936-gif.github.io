🐾 Overview

The Majorca Shepherd Dog (Majorcan Shepherd Dog) is a versatile working breed native to the Balearic Islands of Spain, particularly Mallorca. Used traditionally for herding livestock and guarding farms, this athletic, black-coated dog is known for its intelligence, loyalty, and adaptability. The breed was officially recognized by the FCI and remains relatively rare outside its homeland.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Loyal, alert, and energetic. The Majorca Shepherd Dog bonds deeply with its family and is naturally protective. Reserved with strangers but not aggressive. Makes an excellent watch and working dog.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

High energy: 60–90 minutes daily. Thrives with a job to do. Needs space and mental stimulation to stay happy.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Short or medium black coat. Low-maintenance — weekly brushing and occasional bathing. Check paws and ears after outdoor work.

🎓 Training

Highly intelligent and responsive. Benefits from early socialization and firm, consistent training. Excels in herding, agility, and obedience.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally hardy. Hip dysplasia monitoring recommended. Regular dental care and annual vet checkups advised.

🎉 Fun Facts About Majorca Shepherd Dog

  • "Majorca Shepherd Dog" means "livestock dog" in the Catalan language of Mallorca.
  • One of the oldest dog breeds native to the Balearic Islands.
  • Comes in both short-haired and long-haired varieties.
  • Almost always solid black — a white patch on the chest is permissible.
  • Increasingly popular as a police and search-and-rescue dog in Spain.
