🐾 Overview

The Burgos Pointing Dog (Perdiguero de Burgos) is one of Spain's oldest and most versatile hunting breeds, dating back several centuries. Originally developed in the Castile region, this large pointing dog excels at finding and pointing game birds on all types of terrain. Its calm, docile temperament also makes it a devoted family companion.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Calm, gentle, and obedient. The Burgos Pointing Dog is affectionate with family and patient with children. Highly focused in the field, yet relaxed and manageable at home.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

High energy: 60–90 minutes daily. Needs ample space to run and explore. Ideal for active families or hunters.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Short, dense coat. Low maintenance — weekly brushing and occasional bathing. Check ears regularly for moisture.

🎓 Training

Eager to please and highly trainable. Responds well to positive reinforcement. Natural hunting instincts make field training intuitive.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally healthy. Hip dysplasia screening recommended. Ear infections possible due to pendulous ears. Regular vet checkups advised.

🎉 Fun Facts About Burgos Pointing Dog

  • One of Spain's oldest hunting breeds, with roots dating to the 15th–16th centuries.
  • Named after the city of Burgos in Castile, Spain.
  • Also called "Perdiguero de Burgos" — perdiguero means partridge hunter in Spanish.
  • Known for hunting both fur and feather game with equal skill.
  • Nearly went extinct in the 20th century but was revived by dedicated Spanish breeders.
