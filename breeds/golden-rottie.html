Golden Rottie

Designer Mixed Breed - Golden Retriever x Rottweiler - Loyal Family Guardian

65-110 lbsWeight
22-27 inHeight
9-12 yrsLifespan
Golden, Mahogany, Black and Tan, RedColors

Overview

The Golden Rottie combines the Golden Retriever warmth and trainability with the Rottweiler protective instincts and confidence. The result is a loyal, intelligent large dog that is devoted to family and makes an excellent guardian. Proper socialization from puppyhood is essential.

Origin

Coat

Temperament

Loyal, intelligent, and protective. The Golden warmth typically moderates Rottweiler intensity, creating a balanced family and working dog.

  • Extremely loyal to family
  • Protective but not aggressive
  • Intelligent and trainable
  • Good with kids when socialized
  • May be reserved with strangers

Exercise

Moderate to high - 60-75 minutes daily. Loves fetch from Golden side and working activities from Rottweiler side.

Grooming

Medium coat sheds moderately to heavily. Brush 3-4 times weekly. Professional grooming seasonally helpful.

Training

Highly trainable - Golden eagerness combined with Rottweiler focus creates an exceptional working and obedience dog. Early training from puppyhood essential.

Health

Hip dysplasia from both parents. Rottweiler cancer predisposition plus Golden cancer risk means diligent health screening is important after age 6.

Fun Facts About Golden Rottie

🦺

Natural Guardian

The Rottweiler is one of history oldest guardian breeds; the Golden Retriever is one of the most gentle. Golden Rotties balance these traits into devoted family protectors.

💛

Appearance Surprise

Golden Rotties often look dramatically different even within the same litter - some look like Goldens with dark markings, others like Rotties with golden coats.
