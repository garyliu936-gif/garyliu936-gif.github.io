🐾 Overview

The Atlas Mountain Dog (also called Atlas Mountain Dog, Atlas Mountain Dog) is North Africa's premier livestock guardian, developed in the Atlas Mountains of Morocco to protect sheep and goat herds from large predators including jackals, hyenas, and leopards. The breed is known as the "Atlas Hound" and has served Moroccan shepherds for centuries, valued for its fearlessness, strength, and unwavering loyalty to its flock and family.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Protective, courageous, and fiercely loyal. The Atlas Mountain Dog bonds deeply with its family and livestock while remaining wary of strangers. Independent-minded but responsive to training.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

High energy: 60+ minutes daily of active exercise. Thrives in large spaces. Excellent for herding and livestock work.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Coat varies from short to medium length. Regular brushing; moderate shedding, heavy seasonally.

🎓 Training

Intelligent and trainable but independent. Responds well to consistent, firm leadership. Early socialization is critical.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally hardy. Hip dysplasia possible. Requires annual vet checkups.
