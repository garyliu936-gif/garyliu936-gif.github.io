Borgi

Designer Mixed Breed · Border Collie x Corgi · Herding Compact

Overview

The Borgi crosses the brilliant Border Collie with the charismatic Welsh Corgi. The result is a compact, athletic herding dog packed with intelligence, personality, and enthusiasm. Borgis are wonderful family dogs for active households.

Temperament

Smart, energetic, and friendly. Strong herding instincts from both sides. Loyal and responsive to their families.

  • Highly intelligent and trainable
  • Affectionate with family
  • May herd children and small animals
  • Alert and sometimes protective

Exercise

Needs 60-75 minutes of daily exercise. Thrives with mental stimulation - herding, agility, and obedience training.

Grooming

Brush 2-3 times weekly. Double coat sheds moderately year-round with heavier seasonal shedding.

Training

Highly trainable - inherits intelligence from both parents. Responds enthusiastically to positive reinforcement.

Health

Generally healthy. Monitor for IVDD from Corgi side and eye conditions from Collie side.

Fun Facts

🐑

Herding Powerhouse

Despite being smaller than a Border Collie, Borgis have all the herding drive and intelligence. They take their self-appointed job of herding very seriously.

🦵

Short But Mighty

Borgis may have the Corgi short legs but combine them with Border Collie speed - a surprisingly agile combination.

🌟

Social Media Stars

Borgis have become increasingly popular on social media thanks to their photogenic appearance and entertaining personalities.
