Australian Stumpy Tail Cattle Dog

Cattle Dog / FCI Group 1 · Purebred · Australia's compact bobtailed herder — a tireless stockman with a natural short tail, bred to control cattle in harsh Australian conditions

🐾 Overview

The Australian Stumpy Tail Cattle Dog is a compact, sturdy herding dog developed in Australia specifically for controlling cattle. Distinguished by its naturally short, stubby tail (unlike the closely related Australian Cattle Dog), the Stumpy Tail is a tireless, intelligent worker.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Alert, intelligent, and intensely driven. Excellent herding instinct. Devoted to family but requires work.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

Very high energy: 90+ minutes daily. Needs herding work or intense activity.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Short, dense, mottle or speckle coat. Minimal grooming. Regular brushing; bathe as needed.

🎓 Training

Highly intelligent and trainable. Thrives with herding work. Early socialization critical.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally hardy. Hip and elbow dysplasia screening recommended.
