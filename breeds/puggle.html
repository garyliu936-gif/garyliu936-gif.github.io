Breed Overview

The Puggle is a cross between a Beagle and a Pug — one of the most popular designer dogs of the 2000s. Bred by Wisconsin breeder Wallace Havens in the 1980s and gaining mass popularity in the early 2000s, the Puggle combines the Beagle's longer muzzle and floppy ears with the Pug's stocky body and wrinkled face. The result is a slightly less flat-faced dog than the Pug, which can reduce some breathing difficulties.

Puggles are sociable, playful, and affectionate — wonderful family dogs who are adaptable to apartment or house living. They love people and children and rarely meet a stranger. They can inherit the Beagle's strong scent drive and stubborn streak, so training with food rewards works best. They also tend to howl — a very vocal breed.

Feeding Your Puggle

Puggles are notorious food hounds — both parent breeds are highly food-motivated and prone to obesity. Strict portion control is essential.

Puppy (under 12 mo)¾–1.5 cups350–550 kcal

Nutrition Tips

  • Never free-feed — Puggles will eat until sick
  • Low-calorie treats preserve calorie budget for meals
  • Omega-3 fatty acids reduce skin fold irritation
  • Slow feeders help prevent gulping, which can cause digestive issues

Puggles are one of the most affordable designer breeds to maintain — their short coat is almost zero-maintenance and food costs are moderate. Watch for respiratory vet visits if brachycephalic traits are pronounced.

Puggle Variations & Related Mixes

Puggle Beagle Backcross

Puggle × Beagle. Longer muzzle, strong scent drive, leaner body.

Puggle Pug Backcross

Puggle × Pug. More flat-faced, stockier, classic Pug personality.

Miniature Puggle

Puggle × smaller Beagle or Pug. Under 15 lbs, compact apartment dog.

Puggador

Puggle × Labrador. Friendly, food-loving, highly social family dog.

Fun Facts About Puggles

  • 📺 Puggles became a celebrity sensation in the mid-2000s when Jake Gyllenhaal, Sylvester Stallone, and Uma Thurman were spotted with their Puggles — demand immediately skyrocketed.
  • 🐽 The Puggle's slightly longer muzzle (from the Beagle) often reduces breathing problems compared to purebred Pugs — making them a healthier alternative for fans of flat-faced dogs.
  • 🎵 Puggles are one of the most vocal hybrid breeds — they can bay like a Beagle, snort like a Pug, and produce a remarkable range of sounds that owners describe as "conversational."
  • 🦴 Despite their small size, Puggles have a powerful scenting ability inherited from the Beagle — they will follow their nose relentlessly and should always be leashed outdoors.
  • 🏆 In 2005, the Puggle was named the most popular hybrid dog in the United States by multiple pet industry surveys — temporarily outselling many purebred breeds.
