Overview

The Flat-Coated Goldador crosses two of the most enthusiastic retriever breeds. This double-retriever hybrid is joyful, friendly, athletic, and excellent with families. Two closely related breeds produce very consistent and predictable results.

Temperament

Extremely friendly and enthusiastic. Both parents are among the friendliest breeds in existence. Outstanding family dogs in every situation.

  • Exceptional with children
  • Friendly with everyone
  • High energy and playful
  • Excellent with other dogs

Exercise

High energy - 60-90 minutes daily. Loves retrieving, swimming, and running. An active working breed needing significant daily exercise.

Grooming

Dense coat sheds moderately year-round. Brush 2-3 times weekly. Clean floppy ears regularly to prevent infections.

Training

Highly trainable - two retriever breeds with identical working drive. One of the easiest and most rewarding hybrid dogs to train.

Health

Cancer from Flat-Coat side is a serious concern. Hip and elbow dysplasia from both parent breeds are also common concerns.

Fun Facts About Flat-Coated Goldador

🎾

Retrieve Everything

Both parents were bred for retrieving. Flat-Coated Goldadors may be the most dedicated fetching dogs in existence - they never tire of the game.

💛

Friendliness Overload

Both the Flat-Coat and Lab are famous for friendliness with strangers. The Flat-Coated Goldador has essentially zero stranger caution - everyone is a friend.
