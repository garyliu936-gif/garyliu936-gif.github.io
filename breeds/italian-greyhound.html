Breed Overview

The Italian Greyhound (IG) is a miniaturized sighthound with a history stretching back over 2,000 years to ancient Egypt and Greece. During the Renaissance, they were prized by Italian nobility and depicted in countless paintings. Often called "IGs" by enthusiasts, they combine the speed and athleticism of a sighthound with the size and affection of a lapdog.

IGs are deeply devoted to their families and crave physical contact — they are notorious "velcro dogs" who love to burrow under blankets and curl up against their owners. Despite their delicate appearance, they are fast, agile runners who need regular bursts of exercise. Their short coat offers little insulation, making them cold-sensitive and big fans of sweaters.

Health Notes

  • Leg fractures — thin bones break easily, especially in puppies
  • Progressive retinal atrophy (PRA)
  • Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease
  • Periodontal disease — dental care is critical

Feeding Your Italian Greyhound

IGs are small and naturally lean. Feed a high-quality small-breed formula twice daily. Their metabolism runs fast, so calorie-dense food supports their energy without overloading tiny stomachs.

Life StageDaily AmountCalories/Day
Puppy (under 12 mo)¼–½ cup200–300 kcal
Adult (1–8 yrs)¼–½ cup200–280 kcal
Senior (8+ yrs)¼ cup160–220 kcal

Nutrition Tips

  • Dental health is critical — hard kibble or dental chews help
  • Calcium and phosphorus balance supports their fragile bone structure
  • Avoid obesity — extra weight stresses the fine legs
  • Warm meals in cold weather help these temperature-sensitive dogs

Fun Facts About Italian Greyhounds

  • 👑 Italian Greyhounds were favored by royalty throughout history — King James I, Catherine the Great, and Frederick the Great of Prussia all kept them.
  • 🎨 Renaissance paintings by Pisanello, Velázquez, and Van Eyck all depict Italian Greyhounds lounging beside noble ladies.
  • 💨 Despite their delicate frame, IGs can run up to 25 mph — faster than most medium-sized dogs.
  • 🌡️ They have essentially no body fat and a very thin coat, making them one of the most cold-sensitive breeds — many IG owners have an entire wardrobe of dog sweaters.
  • 🦴 Puppy Italian Greyhounds have remarkably fragile legs — jumping off furniture can cause fractures, so many IG owners use dog stairs everywhere.
