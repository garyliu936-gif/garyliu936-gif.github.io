Overview

The Ori-Pei (also called Miniature Shar-Pei) is a compact cross of the Shar-Pei and Pug. Both brachycephalic breeds with wrinkled faces, the Ori-Pei is a charming but health-conscious companion. The Pug softens the Shar-Pei aloofness while the Shar-Pei adds a compact size. Skin fold and breathing management is essential.

Temperament

Calm, loyal, and devoted. The Shar-Pei loyalty with Pug friendliness creates a balanced wrinkled companion that loves family.

  • Calm and devoted
  • Loyal to family
  • Adapts well indoors
  • Manageable energy

Exercise

Low needs - 20-30 minutes daily. Strict heat avoidance required due to double brachycephalic inheritance from both parents.

Grooming

Short coat is low maintenance. Critical to clean ALL skin folds daily to prevent painful infections - both parents have significant fold concerns.

Training

Moderately trainable. Shar-Pei independence moderated by Pug food motivation. Positive reinforcement with treats works best.

Health

Double brachycephalic risk from both parents is a serious concern. Shar-Pei fever and skin fold pyoderma are additional breed-specific risks.

Fun Facts About Ori-Pei

🧴

Wrinkle Care Ritual

The Ori-Pei combines Shar-Pei rolls and Pug facial folds into significant wrinkle territory. Daily fold cleaning with a soft cloth prevents skin infections that can be painful and serious.

🇨🇳

Chinese Heritage Doubled

Both the Chinese Shar-Pei and the Pug originate from China. The Ori-Pei is a cross of two ancient Chinese companion breeds with thousands of years of recorded history.
