😊 Temperament & Personality

Soft Coated Wheaten Terriers are known for being independent and spirited. They form deep bonds with their families and thrive on consistent human connection. They strike a good balance — lively enough to enjoy playtime and adventures, but calm enough to settle indoors when the day is done.

Soft Coated Wheaten Terriers can be trained with patience and consistency, though they may occasionally test boundaries. With proper socialization they can do well with children, though supervision is always wise.

  • Moderately active — enjoys exercise and playtime but appreciates downtime at home
  • Affectionate and people-oriented — thrives on closeness with their family
  • Generally good with kids when properly socialized from puppyhood
  • Capable learner who benefits from short, varied training sessions
  • Breed-typical personality is reliable and predictable — makes planning your lifestyle together easier
  • Genuinely enjoys relaxing — equally happy on a couch as on a trail with the right owner

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

Soft Coated Wheaten Terriers need regular daily exercise to maintain a healthy weight and an even temperament. They are versatile companions who enjoy active outings but are equally content to relax at home after their needs are met.

  • Daily exercise: 45 – 60 minutes of moderate activity
  • Daily vigorous play, digging opportunities, and interactive games that channel their prey drive
  • Daily walks, play sessions, and occasional trips to a dog park are ideal
  • Mental enrichment (puzzle toys, training) complements physical exercise
  • Adjust intensity based on age — puppies and seniors need gentler, shorter sessions
  • Indoor play can substitute on days with extreme weather

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Soft Coated Wheaten Terriers have a short, low-maintenance coat that requires minimal grooming compared to longer-haired breeds. A consistent basic routine keeps them clean, comfortable, and healthy.

  • Brush once a week with a soft bristle brush or rubber grooming mitt
  • Bathe every 6–8 weeks, or when dirty — overbathing strips natural coat oils
  • Clean ears weekly and check for redness or odor that may indicate infection
  • Trim nails every 3–4 weeks — they often don't wear down naturally indoors
  • Wipe down with a damp cloth between baths to keep the coat gleaming
  • Wire-coated terriers benefit from hand-stripping rather than clipping to maintain coat texture. Smooth-coated varieties need only weekly brushing.

🎓 Training

Soft Coated Wheaten Terriers are moderately trainable and respond well to patient, consistent positive reinforcement. They benefit from clear expectations and a calm, confident trainer who establishes routines early.

Keep sessions short — 10 to 15 minutes — and always end on a success. Enrolling in a puppy or adult obedience class provides structured learning and valuable socialization.

  • Begin training and socialization as early as possible — the puppy window is critical
  • Use high-value rewards (real meat, cheese) to compete with distractions
  • Be consistent — the same rules must apply every session and every family member must agree
  • Terriers are intelligent but independent — short, varied sessions with high-value rewards work best. Avoid repetition, which bores them quickly.
  • Crate training establishes boundaries, aids house training, and gives your dog a safe personal space
  • Short, positive sessions daily beat long, infrequent sessions every time

🏠 Is a Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier Right for You?

A Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier fits well with families or individuals who can provide moderate daily exercise and consistent companionship. They adapt reasonably well to various living situations as long as their exercise and social needs are met. Not ideal for owners away from home for long hours without a pet care solution in place.

Feeding Your Wheaten Terrier

Wheatens typically eat 1.5–2 cups of high-quality dry kibble daily, split into two meals. Food sensitivities are common — many do best on limited-ingredient or novel protein diets.

Life StageDaily AmountMeals/Day
Puppy (2–6 mo)1–1.5 cups3
Adult (1–8 yr)1.5–2 cups2
Senior (9+ yr)1–1.5 cups2

Omega-3 fatty acids support kidney health — especially important given PLN/PLE risk. Avoid high-sodium diets if kidney concerns arise. Consult your vet about protein levels appropriate for your individual dog.

Popular Wheaten Terrier Mixes

Whoodle (Wheaten + Poodle)

A low-shedding, highly intelligent mix combining the Wheaten's fun personality with Poodle brains. One of the most popular Wheaten mixes — cheerful, trainable, and allergy-friendly.

Wheaten-Labrador Mix

The Lab's trainability meets the Wheaten's silky coat and spirited energy. A friendly, medium-sized family dog that's often lower-shedding than a purebred Lab.

Wheaten-Cocker Spaniel Mix

A gentle, affectionate mix with beautiful wavy fur. Inherits the Wheaten's low-shedding qualities alongside the Cocker's sweet, loyal disposition.

Fun Facts About Wheaten Terriers

  • 🌾 All-Season Coat: The Wheaten's single-layer coat doesn't shed like a double coat — instead, loose hairs are trapped and tangled in, which is why brushing is so important but shedding is so minimal.
  • 🐕 The Wheaten Greeting: The characteristic leap-and-lick hello Wheatens give to everyone they meet is so famous it has its own name — the "Wheaten greeting." Training "four on the floor" behavior takes consistent effort.
  • 🇮🇪 Ancient Irish Roots: Wheatens were the dogs of Irish tenant farmers for centuries — too poor to afford purebred hunting hounds, farmers bred these versatile dogs who could hunt, herd, guard, and kill vermin equally well.
  • 🎨 Born Dark: Wheaten puppies are born with dark brown or black coats that gradually lighten over their first two years to the adult wheat color — confusing to new owners who wonder if they got the right breed.
  • 🌀 Coat Varieties: There are two officially recognized coat varieties — the Irish coat (thinner, silkier, waves loosely) and the American coat (fuller, thicker waves). Both are equally valid but have slightly different grooming needs.
