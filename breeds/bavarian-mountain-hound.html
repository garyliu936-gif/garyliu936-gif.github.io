Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound

Scent Hound / FCI Group 6 · Purebred · Germany's Alpine tracker — a medium-sized red-brown hound specializing in tracking wounded game through mountains with unmatched determination and nose

🐾 Overview

The Bavarian Mountain Scent Hound is a specialized German scent hound developed specifically for tracking wounded game in Alpine mountains. Bred for centuries in Bavaria, these dogs are known for their exceptional nose, determination, and ability to work in challenging mountainous terrain.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Determined, focused, and intensely driven to work. Excellent problem-solver. Devoted to owner but independent hunter.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

High energy: 60+ minutes daily. Bred for Alpine tracking; needs challenging terrain and serious activity.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Short, dense coat. Minimal grooming. Regular brushing; bathe as needed.

🎓 Training

Intelligent and responsive to dedicated training. Bred for independent tracking decisions.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally hardy. Hip dysplasia screening recommended.
