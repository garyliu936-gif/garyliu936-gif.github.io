🐾 Overview

The Chart Polski (Polish Greyhound) is one of the oldest sighthound breeds in Eastern Europe, with a history intertwined with the Polish nobility for centuries. The breed was prized by the szlachta (Polish noble class) as a prestige hunting dog used for coursing hare, fox, and wolf across the open plains and forests of Poland. Its origins likely trace to Oriental sighthounds brought to Eastern Europe via trade and conquest routes, subsequently developed into a distinct Polish type adapted to the demanding Central European climate and terrain.

The Chart Polski is larger, heavier-boned, and more muscular than the English Greyhound, with a slightly rough, semi-long coat that provides additional weather protection. It comes in virtually all colors and color combinations. Classified in FCI Group 10, it is recognized as Poland's national sighthound breed. The Chart Polski is notable for its independence, courage, and intense loyalty to its owner — qualities that make it both fascinating and challenging to own.

😊 Temperament & Personality

The Chart Polski has a proud, independent temperament that reflects its noble heritage. It is deeply loyal to its owner and immediate family but characteristically aloof — even cold — with strangers. It is not a dog that warms up instantly to new people, but those who earn its trust are rewarded with unwavering loyalty.

  • 🦅 Proud and independent — thinks for itself, especially in the field
  • ❤️ Intensely loyal and devoted to its owner and close family
  • 🧍 Aloof and reserved with strangers — not aggressive, simply dignified
  • 💪 Courageous — historically used on wolf, not just hare
  • 🐈 Very high prey drive — unreliable with cats and small animals
  • 🐕 Can coexist with other dogs, especially other sighthounds, with proper introduction

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

The Chart Polski is a high-energy sighthound that requires daily vigorous exercise and regular opportunities to run at speed. A long daily walk is not sufficient — this breed needs to stretch its legs at a full gallop in a safe, enclosed area regularly.

  • 🏃 Needs daily exercise including at least one full-speed run in a secure area
  • 🔒 A tall, fully enclosed fence is essential — Chart Polskis can clear typical garden fences
  • 🌾 Lure coursing and straight racing are ideal sports for this breed
  • 🛋️ After vigorous exercise, Chart Polskis are calm and content indoors
  • 🏆 Participates in lure coursing, racing, and sighthound-specific dog sports
  • ⚠️ Never let off-leash in unsecured areas — prey drive overrides all recall

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

The Chart Polski has a short to semi-long coat that is slightly harder in texture than the English Greyhound's smooth coat. It provides better weather protection but still requires minimal grooming overall. The breed sheds moderately.

  • 🖌️ Brush weekly with a slicker brush or rubber mitt to remove loose hair
  • 🛁 Bathe every 6–8 weeks or as needed
  • 🧥 Provide a coat or jacket in very cold or wet weather — sighthound body composition offers little insulation
  • 🦷 Brush teeth 2–3 times per week; dental health is important for all sighthounds
  • ✂️ Trim nails monthly; check paw pads after coursing for cuts or abrasions
  • 👂 Check and clean ears weekly for debris and early signs of infection

🎓 Training

The Chart Polski is an intelligent but strongly independent breed. It was bred to make autonomous coursing decisions — not to await commands — and this independence carries through to training. Patience, consistency, and positive methods are essential; force or harsh correction will damage the relationship permanently.

  • ✅ Positive reinforcement with high-value food rewards is the most effective approach
  • ⏳ Patience is key — progress is slower than with retriever breeds but results are lasting
  • 🧒 Intensive early socialization is critical to prevent excessive aloofness or fearfulness
  • 🐕 Recall training must be practiced in fully enclosed areas — never assume reliable recall near prey
  • 💪 Requires a calm, confident owner who doesn't become frustrated by independent behavior
  • 🏆 Excels in lure coursing, which naturally channels its instincts productively

🏥 Health & Common Issues

The Chart Polski is a relatively hardy and healthy breed. Like all sighthounds, it has unique physiology including lower body fat percentage and atypical blood values that veterinarians must be aware of — particularly regarding anesthesia protocols. Owners should always inform their vet that their dog is a sighthound before any surgical procedure.

🏠 Is a Chart Polski Right for You?

The Chart Polski is a breed for experienced, patient, and dedicated owners who appreciate the unique sighthound character. It is not a dog for everyone — its independence, high prey drive, and reserved nature toward strangers demand an owner who understands and respects these traits. Given the right home, it is a magnificent, loyal, and deeply rewarding companion.

🍽️ How Much to Feed a Chart Polski

Chart Polskis are large, lean, muscular sighthounds with high metabolisms. Their diet should be rich in high-quality protein to maintain muscle and support their athletic build. Because sighthounds carry very little body fat, weight monitoring is important — they should look lean but not gaunt.

Puppy (8–12 weeks)
3–4 small meals per day
Puppy (3–6 months)
3 meals per day
Adult (1+ year)
2 meals per day
Senior (7+ years)
2 smaller meals per day

✅ Best Foods for Chart Polskis

  • 🥩 High-protein kibble or raw diet with lean meats (chicken, venison, or fish) as the base
  • 🐟 Omega-3 fish oil supplements support joint health and the slightly rough coat
  • ⚖️ Feed to maintain a lean but muscular physique — some rib visibility is normal for this breed
  • ⏱️ Two meals daily and a rest period of at least 1 hour before and after exercise helps prevent bloat
  • 💧 Always ensure access to fresh, clean water — especially after intense exercise sessions

🚫 Foods That Are Dangerous for Chart Polskis

Chocolate Grapes & Raisins Onions & Garlic Xylitol (artificial sweetener) Macadamia Nuts Alcohol Avocado Raw yeast dough

🦴 Healthy Treats

  • Carrots — low calorie, great for dental health
  • Blueberries — antioxidants and a sweet reward
  • Plain cooked chicken or turkey (no seasoning)
  • Apple slices (remove seeds and core)
  • Plain rice cakes — low-calorie training reward

💡 Tip: Boarding your Chart Polski?

Always bring your Chart Polski's regular food when boarding. Switching food suddenly can cause digestive upset.

🎉 Amazing Facts About Chart Polskis

  • 👑 The Chart Polski was historically a breed of the nobility — commoners in medieval Poland were forbidden from owning one.
  • 🐺 Unlike most sighthounds used only for hare and fox, the Chart Polski was also historically used to course wolves — a testament to its exceptional courage and size.
  • 📜 The breed is depicted in Polish noble coats of arms, paintings, and literature stretching back centuries, reflecting its deep cultural significance.
  • 🌍 The Chart Polski was nearly wiped out during the upheavals of the 20th century (WWI, WWII, and communist-era Poland) and was carefully rebuilt by dedicated Polish breeders.
  • 🧬 The Chart Polski is genetically and physically distinct from the English Greyhound — it is heavier, more muscular, and has a slightly rougher coat suited to the Central European climate.
  • 💉 Like all sighthounds, Chart Polskis are sensitive to barbiturate anesthesia — always inform your veterinarian before any surgical procedure.
  • 🏆 The breed competes internationally in lure coursing and sighthound racing events, where its raw power and stamina set it apart from lighter sighthound breeds.
