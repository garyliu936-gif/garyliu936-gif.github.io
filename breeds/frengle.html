Frengle

Designer Mixed Breed - French Bulldog x Beagle - Charming and Social

Overview

The Frengle combines the French Bulldog compact charm with the Beagle friendliness and nose. Social, playful, and great with families. Heat care is essential due to Frenchie brachycephalic traits.

Temperament

Friendly, playful, and social. Frengles love everyone they meet - one of the friendliest small hybrid combinations available.

  • Excellent with children
  • Friendly with everyone
  • Playful and fun
  • Scent distracted outdoors

Exercise

Moderate needs - 30-45 minutes daily. Avoid heat and humidity - brachycephalic traits from the French Bulldog side can cause breathing difficulties in heat.

Grooming

Easy care - brush weekly with rubber mitt. Clean facial folds if inherited from Frenchie side to prevent skin infections.

Training

Moderately trainable but can be stubborn from both sides. Food rewards and short engaging sessions work best.

Health

Brachycephalic concerns from Frenchie and IVDD risk from both sides are primary concerns. Ear infections common from Beagle side.

Fun Facts About Frengle

😤

Bat Ears vs Floppy Ears

The French Bulldog has upright bat ears; the Beagle has long floppy ears. Frengles can inherit either or a mix of both - always a cute surprise!

👃

Scent Hound in a Frenchie Body

The Beagle nose in a compact Frenchie body creates a curious dog that will follow interesting smells anywhere. Always use a leash or secure fencing.
