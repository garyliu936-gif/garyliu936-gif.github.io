Keeshond Shepherd

Designer Mixed Breed - Keeshond x German Shepherd - Alert and Sociable

45-85 lbsWeight
20-26 inHeight
10-13 yrsLifespan
Gray, Sable, Black and Tan, SilverColors

Overview

The Keeshond Shepherd combines the Keeshond warmth with the German Shepherd intelligence and versatility. This medium-to-large hybrid is an alert, trainable companion that is excellent with families. The German Shepherd working drive is balanced by the Keeshond sociable companion nature.

Origin
United States
Parents
Keeshond x German Shepherd
AKC Status
Not AKC registered
Coat
Dense, plush double coat; heavy shedding

Temperament

Alert, sociable, and loyal. Keeshond warmth moderates Shepherd intensity, creating a well-balanced family companion with guarding instincts.

  • Alert and protective
  • Sociable and friendly
  • Highly intelligent
  • Loyal to family

Exercise

Moderate to high - 60-75 minutes daily. Both breeds are active and need varied exercise and mental stimulation to be content.

Grooming

Heavy shedder - dense coat requires brushing 3-4 times weekly and significant grooming during seasonal shed.

Training

Highly trainable thanks to Shepherd intelligence. The Keeshond willingness makes this a particularly responsive and rewarding hybrid to train.

Health

Hip dysplasia from both parent breeds is the primary concern. Addison disease from Keeshond side and DM from Shepherd side are also watch points.
