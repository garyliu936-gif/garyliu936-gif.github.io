Blue Picardy Spaniel

Flushing Dog / FCI Group 8 · Purebred · France's elegant blue-ticked spaniel — a medium-sized flushing dog with distinctive blue mottling bred for hunting waterfowl with grace and style

🐾 Overview

The Blue Picardy Spaniel is a French flushing dog from the Picardy region known for its distinctive blue mottled coat with orange or liver patches. Developed for hunting waterfowl, this breed combines elegant appearance with serious hunting ability.

Real Blue Picardy Spaniel photos — showcasing their unique appearance and character.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Energetic, determined, and eager to please. Excellent hunting drive. Friendly and trainable with proper socialization.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

High energy: 60+ minutes daily. Bred for hunting; thrives with active owners.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Medium-length coat. Regular brushing; professional grooming occasionally.

🎓 Training

Intelligent and responsive. Eager to please. Thrives with hunting training.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally healthy. Hip dysplasia screening recommended.
