Overview

The Borzoi Greyhound is perhaps the ultimate sighthound cross - combining two of the fastest and most elegant coursing breeds. This hybrid is calm indoors (both breeds are couch potato sighthounds at home) but explosive when running. Gentle, sensitive, and strikingly beautiful.

Temperament

Gentle, independent, and reserved. Like all sighthounds, calm at home but instinctively reactive to movement. Bonds closely with family but aloof with strangers.

  • Gentle and affectionate with family
  • Independent and not demanding
  • Strong prey drive - dangerous around small animals
  • Reserved with strangers

Exercise

Sprint-based rather than endurance. Short, fast off-leash runs in a secure area satisfy their needs. Daily exercise of 30-45 minutes plus safe running.

Grooming

Easier than a purebred Borzoi. Brush 2x weekly. Minimal bathing needed. The coat is relatively self-cleaning.

Training

Independent sighthounds are not obedience champions. Recall training is critical. Use gentle positive reinforcement - sighthounds are sensitive.

Health

Generally very healthy and long-lived for large breeds. Bloat is the primary serious concern.

Fun Facts

🌪️

Faster Than You Think

Both parent breeds can reach 40+ mph. Borzoi Greyhounds may be the fastest of all domestic dog hybrids.

🛋️

Olympic Couch Athletes

Despite their speed, both sighthound parents are famously lazy indoors. Borzoi Greyhounds are equally happy sprinting and napping.

👑

Royal Heritage

The Borzoi was the Russian aristocracy hunting breed, the Greyhound adorns Egyptian pharaoh tombs. This is a uniquely noble cross.
