Breed Overview

The Black and Tan Coonhound is a quintessentially American breed, developed in the Appalachian Mountains from crosses of Bloodhounds and Black and Tan Foxhounds. Bred specifically to track and tree raccoons, opossums, and bears by night, they have one of the keenest noses in the dog world — second only to the Bloodhound in scenting ability. Their deep, melodious bay was specifically cultivated to carry through dense forest to guide hunters to the prey.

Off duty, the Black and Tan is a gentle, easygoing companion — calm indoors, devoted to family, and wonderful with children. They tend to be independent thinkers with their nose, which can make training a test of patience. They need a securely fenced yard, as they will follow a scent trail indefinitely if given the chance.

Health Notes

  • Hip and elbow dysplasia
  • Ear infections — long, floppy ears trap moisture
  • Ectropion — drooping lower eyelids
  • Bloat (GDV) — deep-chested breed risk

Feeding Your Black and Tan Coonhound

Large and athletic when active, Black and Tan Coonhounds are calm indoors and can gain weight if exercise is limited. A measured, quality diet prevents obesity.

Life StageDaily AmountCalories/Day
Puppy (under 18 mo)2–3.5 cups900–1,400 kcal
Adult (1.5–7 yrs)3–4 cups1,200–1,600 kcal
Senior (7+ yrs)2–3 cups900–1,200 kcal

Nutrition Tips

  • Large-breed formula supports joint health for their heavy frame
  • Feed twice daily to reduce bloat risk
  • Elevated feeders may help comfort but debate exists on bloat prevention
  • Watch ear hygiene — bacteria thrives in damp ears after drinking

Fun Facts About Black and Tan Coonhounds

  • 🌙 Coonhounds were specifically developed for night hunting — their keen nose and loud bay allowed hunters to track them through dense forest in the dark.
  • 📚 The classic children's novel "Where the Red Fern Grows" features two Redbone Coonhounds, but the Black and Tan is the most historically famous of all coonhound varieties.
  • 🏛️ The Black and Tan Coonhound was the first coonhound variety recognized by the AKC, receiving recognition in 1945.
  • 👃 Their scenting ability is second only to the Bloodhound — they can track a trail that is days old across difficult terrain and in adverse weather.
  • 🎵 The Black and Tan Coonhound's bay was selectively bred to be loud enough to be heard a mile away through thick forest — neighbors in suburban settings will definitely notice.
