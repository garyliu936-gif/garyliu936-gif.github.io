Australian Terrier Diet & Nutrition Guide

🐶 Puppy (0–12 months)

Approach: 3–4 meals daily of small-breed puppy food

Calories: ⅓–½ cup per day total

🐕 Adult (1–7 years)

Approach: 2 meals daily; monitor for diabetes tendency

Calories: ½–¾ cup per day

🦮 Senior (7+ years)

Approach: 2 meals daily; low-glycemic senior formula

Calories: ⅓–½ cup per day

Key Nutritional Priorities

  • High-quality protein for an active small dog
  • Low-glycemic carbohydrates to manage diabetes risk
  • Omega-3s for rough coat condition
  • Calcium for small-breed bone health

Fun Facts About the Australian Terrier

  • Historically used to kill venomous snakes in the Australian bush
  • Developed from a mix of Dandie Dinmont, Scottish, Yorkshire, and Cairn Terriers
  • Their keen hearing makes them exceptional watchdogs
  • Often described as having the courage of a dog three times their size
