Overview

The Arctic Labrador blends the Alaskan Malamute's power and endurance with the Labrador's beloved friendliness. This athletic hybrid thrives in active families and cold climates, combining the Lab's eagerness to please with the Malamute's impressive working ability.

Temperament

Friendly, loyal, and energetic. More trainable than a purebred Malamute due to the Lab's eagerness to please. Pack-oriented and social.

  • Friendly and social
  • Loyal to family
  • High energy
  • Prey drive from Malamute side

Exercise

Needs 60-90 minutes of vigorous daily exercise. Built for cold weather and distance - hiking and running are ideal.

Grooming

Brush 3-4 times weekly. Heavy seasonal shedding requires daily brushing during blow season.

Training

More trainable than purebred Malamutes. Lab intelligence and eagerness help. Start early with consistent positive reinforcement.

Health

Generally healthy and hardy. Hip dysplasia and eye conditions are the main concerns from both sides.

Fun Facts About Arctic Labrador

❄️

Cold Weather Expert

The Malamute parent was bred to haul freight in Arctic temperatures. Arctic Labs are naturally suited to cold climates.

🦮

Working Heritage

Both parents are working breeds - Labs as retrievers, Malamutes as sled dogs. Arctic Labs need a job to feel fulfilled.

🌊

Love Water Too

Despite the Malamute heritage, most Arctic Labs inherit the Lab love of water and swimming.
