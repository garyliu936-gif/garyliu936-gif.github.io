Bull Labrador

Designer Mixed Breed - Bull Terrier x Labrador - Energetic Companion

Overview

The Bull Labrador combines the Bull Terrier's energy and tenacity with the Labrador's irresistible friendliness. The result is a muscular, loyal companion that is devoted to family and benefits greatly from early socialization and consistent training.

Temperament

Energetic, loyal, and playful. Lab friendliness usually moderates Bull Terrier intensity. Devoted to family with proper socialization.

  • Loyal and devoted
  • High energy
  • Can be stubborn
  • Early socialization critical

Exercise

High energy - 60-90 minutes daily of vigorous exercise including fetch and running.

Grooming

Easy care - brush weekly with rubber mitt. Bathe every 4-6 weeks.

Training

Trainable but can be stubborn from Bull Terrier side. Consistent positive reinforcement from puppyhood.

Health

Generally healthy hybrid. Skin conditions and heart problems from Bull Terrier side are concerns.
