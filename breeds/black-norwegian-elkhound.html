🐾 Overview

The Black Norwegian Elkhound is a Norwegian hunting dog specializing in pursuing moose and other large game. Descended from ancient Scandinavian spitz types, this breed combines the robust build of a working hunter with the independent nature of a northern hunting dog.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Determined, independent, and intensely driven to hunt. Bold and courageous. Loyal to family but bred for autonomous work.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

High energy: 60+ minutes daily. Bred for hunting in forests; needs very active owners.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Double coat. Regular brushing essential, especially during shedding seasons.

🎓 Training

Intelligent but independent-minded. Responds to firm, consistent leadership.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally hardy. Hip dysplasia screening recommended.
