Overview

The Tervuren Shepherd combines two elite Belgian and German herding breeds. Both the Belgian Tervuren and German Shepherd are used extensively in police, military, and competition work. This hybrid is highly intelligent, driven, and loyal - requiring experienced ownership with purposeful activity.

Temperament

Driven, loyal, and highly intelligent. Two elite working herding breeds combine into an exceptional working and family dog for experienced handlers.

  • Highly intelligent
  • Intensely loyal
  • Working dog drive
  • Needs experienced handler

Exercise

High energy - 75-90 minutes daily. Both parent breeds are elite working dogs requiring substantial exercise and mental work.

Grooming

Heavy coat sheds significantly. Brush 3-4 times weekly and daily during seasonal blow. Professional grooming seasonally.

Training

Highly trainable - two of the most intelligent herding breeds. Excels at obedience, agility, herding, protection, and SAR work.

Health

Hip dysplasia from both parents. Degenerative myelopathy from Shepherd. Epilepsy from Tervuren. All working-breed concerns apply.

Fun Facts About Tervuren Shepherd

🇧🇪

Belgian and German Excellence

Belgium and Germany both produced elite herding and working breeds independently. The Tervuren Shepherd unites these two great working dog traditions in a single remarkable hybrid.

🎯

Competition Ready

Both parent breeds consistently dominate working dog competitions. Tervuren Shepherds have the genetic potential to compete at elite levels in virtually any dog sport.
