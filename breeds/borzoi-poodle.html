Borzoi Poodle

Designer Mixed Breed - Borzoi x Poodle - Elegant and Smart

Overview

The Borzoi Poodle combines the Borzoi's aristocratic beauty and sighthound speed with the Poodle's intelligence and low-shedding coat. Striking in appearance and surprisingly trainable for a sighthound cross.

Photo Gallery

Temperament

Gentle, independent, and intelligent. More trainable than a purebred Borzoi. Quiet and calm indoors with bursts of outdoor energy.

  • Gentle with family
  • Independent streak
  • High prey drive
  • Calm indoors

Exercise

Moderate needs with speed bursts. 45-60 minutes daily including off-leash sprinting in a secure area.

Grooming

Wavy coat needs brushing 2-3 times weekly. Professional grooming every 8-10 weeks helps manage coat.

Training

More trainable than purebred Borzoi thanks to Poodle intelligence. Gentle positive reinforcement works best.

Health

Bloat is the primary serious concern given both parents have deep chests. Regular health screening recommended.

Fun Facts About Borzoi Poodle

🎨

Museum-Worthy Beauty

Borzoi Poodles consistently produce visually spectacular hybrid dogs, combining flowing coats with elegant proportions.

🧠

The Smart Sighthound

Poodle intelligence gives Borzoi Poodles trainability the Borzoi alone lacks, making them far more responsive to commands.

🏃

Zero to Sixty

Like all sighthound crosses, Borzoi Poodles explode from rest to full speed instantly. Most dog owners are shocked the first time they see it.
