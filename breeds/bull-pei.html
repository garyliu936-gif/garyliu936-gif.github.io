Bull-Pei

Designer Mixed Breed - Bulldog x Chinese Shar-Pei - Wrinkly Companion

14-18 inHeight

Overview

The Bull-Pei is an adorably wrinkly hybrid combining the Bulldog's calm affection with the Chinese Shar-Pei's loyalty and distinctive appearance. This is a low-energy dog that thrives in apartment settings but requires diligent care of its skin folds.

Temperament

Calm, loyal, and somewhat stubborn. Reserved with strangers from Shar-Pei side, sweet with family. Not particularly playful but very affectionate.

  • Loyal to family
  • Reserved with strangers
  • Low energy
  • Stubborn streak

Exercise

Low to moderate needs - 30-45 minutes of gentle walks daily. Be very careful in heat due to brachycephalic traits.

Grooming

Most important care item is cleaning all skin folds daily to prevent infections. Brush weekly otherwise.

Training

Stubborn from both sides. Keep sessions short, use high-value treats, and be very consistent. Not the most trainable hybrid.

Health

Skin fold infections are a daily management issue. Brachycephalic breathing concerns from Bulldog side require vet monitoring.

Fun Facts

😤

Wrinkle Champions

Both parent breeds are famous for their wrinkled appearance. Bull-Peis can have extraordinary amounts of skin folds - adorable but requiring diligent daily care.

🛋️

Champion Loungers

Bull-Peis inherit the Bulldog legendary love of lounging combined with Shar-Pei's calm dignity. They are content couch companions who don't demand constant exercise.
