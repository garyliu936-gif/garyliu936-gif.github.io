🐾 Overview

The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel is one of Britain's most beloved companion breeds, descended from the small toy spaniels favored by royalty in the 16th–17th centuries. Named after King Charles II, who was rarely seen without his spaniels, the modern Cavalier was revived in the 1920s to recreate the elegant, flat-faced dogs seen in old royal portraits. Today it ranks among the most popular family dogs worldwide — gentle, adaptable, and deeply affectionate.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Sweet, gentle, and extremely sociable. The Cavalier thrives on human companionship and gets along with everyone — children, other dogs, and strangers alike. Eager to please and adaptable to most living situations.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

Moderate energy: 30–45 minutes daily. Enjoys walks and play but is equally happy snuggling indoors. Versatile — suits apartments and houses alike.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Silky, medium-length coat with feathering on ears, chest, and legs. Brush 3–4 times per week to prevent tangles. Regular ear cleaning essential; check eyes frequently.

🎓 Training

Eager to please and highly trainable. Responds beautifully to gentle, positive reinforcement. Sensitive — avoid harsh corrections. Excels in obedience, agility, and therapy dog work.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Prone to mitral valve heart disease (MVD) — the most significant health concern; screen breeding dogs. Also watch for syringomyelia (SM), hip dysplasia, and eye conditions. Choose breeders who follow health testing protocols.

🧬 Popular Cavalier King Charles Spaniel Mix Breeds

Cavalier King Charles Spaniel are sometimes crossed with other breeds to create unique companions that combine the best traits of both parents. Here are some of the most popular mixes.

Cavapoo

Parents: Cavalier + Poodle

One of the most popular designer dogs — gentle, low-shedding, and irresistibly affectionate.

Cavachon

Parents: Cavalier + Bichon Frisé

Sweet and sociable, this soft-coated mix is ideal for families and apartment living alike.

Cavapoochon

Parents: Cavalier + Poodle + Bichon Frisé

Triple-cross that combines the best traits of three beloved companion breeds — gentle, smart, and low-shed.

🎉 Fun Facts About Cavalier King Charles Spaniel

  • Named after King Charles II of England, who was rarely seen without his toy spaniels.
  • Appeared in many 17th-century royal portraits alongside monarchs and noblewomen.
  • The breed was nearly lost — revived in the 1920s by American Roswell Eldridge who offered a cash prize to breeders recreating the old-style spaniel.
  • One of the largest toy breeds — sometimes called the "love sponge" of dogs.
  • Consistently ranks in the top 20 most popular breeds in the UK and US.
