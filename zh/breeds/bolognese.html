🐾 Overview

The Bolognese is an Italian toy dog with ancient roots among European nobility. This small, fluffy white companion dog was bred specifically as a lapdog and status symbol for Italian aristocracy, valued for its affectionate nature and low exercise requirements.

📸 Photo Gallery

Real Bolognese photos — showcasing their unique appearance and character.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Calm, gentle, and devoted. Excellent with families. Quiet and undemanding. Dislikes separation from owners.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

Low–Moderate: 20–30 minutes daily. Short walks and indoor play sufficient.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Long, white coat requires regular brushing (3–4 times weekly). Professional grooming recommended.

🎓 Training

Intelligent and eager to please. Responds well to positive reinforcement.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally healthy. Dental care important. Regular vet checkups.
