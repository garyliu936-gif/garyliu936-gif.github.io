🐾 Overview

The Auvergne Pointer is a French pointer from the Auvergne region known for its distinctive black and white parti-colored coat. Developed for bird hunting, this versatile pointer combines hunting ability with a calm, trainable temperament.

📸 Photo Gallery

Real Auvergne Pointer photos — showcasing their unique appearance and character.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Energetic, determined, and eager to please. Excellent hunting instinct. Gentle and trainable with proper socialization.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

High energy: 60+ minutes daily. Bred for hunting; thrives with active owners.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Short, dense coat. Minimal grooming. Regular brushing; bathe as needed.

🎓 Training

Intelligent and responsive. Eager to please. Thrives with hunting training.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Generally hardy. Hip dysplasia screening recommended.
