🐾 Overview

The Norman Artesian Basset is a French short-legged scent hound developed specifically for hunting rabbits. This breed combines the iconic basset body type with French hunting heritage, resulting in a dog that is both an excellent hunter and a delightful companion.

📸 Photo Gallery

Real Norman Artesian Basset photos — showcasing their unique appearance and character.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Friendly, laid-back, and driven to hunt. Excellent nose and stubborn independence. Good with family and children.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

Moderate–High: 30–60 minutes daily of gentle exercise. Bred for hunting; loves to follow scents.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Short coat. Minimal grooming needs. Regular brushing; watch for ear infections.

🎓 Training

Intelligent but independent-minded. Responds to patient, consistent training.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Watch for ear infections. Hip dysplasia screening recommended. Back issues possible.
