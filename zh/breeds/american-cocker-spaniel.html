🐾 Overview

The American Cocker Spaniel is the refined American descendant of the English Cocker Spaniel, developed in the United States to be both an effective flushing dog and a loving family companion. Smaller and longer-coated than their English cousins, American Cockers have become one of the most popular toy and companion breeds worldwide.

😊 Temperament & Personality

Gentle, eager to please, and affectionate. Excellent with children and families. Love water and retrieving. Generally friendly even with strangers.

🏃 Exercise & Activity Needs

Moderate–High: 30–60 minutes daily. Enjoy walks, play, and water activities. Need regular mental stimulation.

✂️ Grooming & Coat Care

Silky, medium-length coat requires regular brushing (3–4 times weekly). Professional grooming every 6–8 weeks. Ear care essential.

🎓 Training

Highly intelligent and eager to please. Respond well to positive reinforcement. Early socialization important.

🏥 Health & Common Issues

Watch for: ear infections, hip dysplasia, eye conditions. Regular vet checkups and genetic screening recommended.
