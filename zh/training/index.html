The Foundation

5 Rules Every Dog Trainer Lives By

Before you teach a single command, these principles determine whether training succeeds or fails.

1
🔄

Be Consistent

Use the exact same word and hand signal every time. Everyone in the house must use the same cue — dogs can't understand synonyms.

2
🎯

Reward Immediately

You have a 2-second window. The treat must come within 2 seconds of the correct behavior — otherwise your dog connects the reward to what they did last.

3
⏱️

Keep Sessions Short

5–10 minutes, 2–3 times a day. Always end on a success so your dog feels confident. Marathon sessions lead to frustration, not learning.

4
😊

Stay Positive

Positive reinforcement works faster and builds a stronger bond than punishment. Never punish a dog for not understanding — it creates fear, not learning.

5
📈

Raise the Bar Gradually

Once your dog gets a command right 8/10 times, add a distraction or new location. This "proofing" is what makes commands stick for life.
